FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What's included in the kit?

The Classic Navy Kit comes with a navy EVA hard-shell case, 5 wood golf tees engraved with “Swing the balance”, a divot tool, 2 magnetic hat-clip ball markers, a glove holder and a carabiner to clip the case to your bag or cart.

Will the case keep my accessories dry?

The durable EVA hard shell helps protect against water and dust, and its ventilated design helps reduce moisture buildup inside. It isn't meant to be submerged.

Do you ship across the US?

Yes. Scatto ships within the United States. We'll confirm delivery times and shipping costs with you when you place your order.

How do I order a kit?

Email us at contact@scattogolf.com with the kit and quantity you're interested in. We'll reply with availability, pricing and next steps.

Can I order kits for a company, tournament or event?

Absolutely. Email us at contact@scattogolf.com with the quantity and the date you need them by, and we'll put together a proposal.

Can you add our logo to the kits?

Ask us about custom branding. Include your logo and quantities in your message and we'll let you know what's possible.

Do the kits make good gifts?

They're a practical gift for Father's Day, birthdays or any occasion worth celebrating: useful on every round and presented in a case that looks great from the first moment. Email us and let us know the date you need it by.

What if something isn't right with my order?

Email us at contact@scattogolf.com and we'll help you sort it out.
