FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you ship across the US?

Yes. Scatto ships within the United States. We'll confirm delivery times and shipping costs with you when you place your order.

How do I order a kit?

Send us an inquiry using the form above with the kit and quantity you're interested in. We'll reply with availability, pricing and next steps.

Can I order kits for a company, tournament or event?

Absolutely. Choose “Corporate / event” in the form and tell us the quantity and the date you need them by, and we'll put together a proposal.

Can you add our logo to the kits?

Ask us about custom branding. Include your logo and quantities in your message and we'll let you know what's possible.

Do the kits make good gifts?

They're one of our favorite gifts for golfers: useful on every round and presented in a case that looks great from the first moment.

What if something isn't right with my order?

Get in touch through this page and we'll help you sort it out.
