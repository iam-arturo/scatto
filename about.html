About Scatto

Swing the balance

Golf is a game of balance: power and control, focus and fun. We make the kit that helps you keep it.

Why we started

Tees in one pocket, a ball marker in another, the divot tool lost somewhere at the bottom of the bag. Every golfer knows the feeling.

Scatto was born from that small frustration. We wanted one place for the little things that make a round go smoothly, designed with the same care golfers put into their game.

Our squirrel says it best: keep what matters close, and keep it organized. That way, all your attention goes to the next shot.
